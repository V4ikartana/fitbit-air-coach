# Code of conduct

This project is a small, friendly space for people who want to understand their own health data. Everyone who takes
part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews, is asked to keep it that way.

## What we ask

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that seem basic.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's health data, personal details or private messages, and take care with
  your own.
- Stay on topic, and don't present opinions as medical advice.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or demeaning language about anyone's identity, body or health.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly continuing a conversation after being asked to stop.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
warn, temporarily block or permanently block anyone who does so. They will act fairly and keep reports confidential.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer, in either of these ways:

- open a private advisory with "Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab of
  https://github.com/v4ikartana/fitbit-air-coach (the same private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)),
  and say that it is a conduct report;
- or open an issue at https://github.com/v4ikartana/fitbit-air-coach/issues asking for a private contact channel,
  without including any details of the problem.

If you can't use these channels, or the report concerns the maintainer, report it to GitHub instead:
https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

It is written for this project and adapted in spirit from common community codes of conduct.
